# Nice Dice – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 16, 2026

This policy covers **Nice Dice** (`com.ominom.nicedice`), an Android game
by Michele Miotti, available on Google Play.

## In short

Nice Dice does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal
data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app
does not even have permission to access the internet.

## Data stored on your device

To work, the game saves the following **only on your device**:

- your games in progress and their undo history
- your high scores
- your settings, such as whether sound is muted

This data never leaves your device. It is not included in Android cloud
backups, and nobody, including the developer, can access it.

## Deleting your data

Since all data is on your device, you are in full control of it. To
delete it, either uninstall Nice Dice, or go to **Settings → Apps → Nice
Dice → Storage → Clear storage** on your device.

## Donation link

The game has an optional "donate" button. Tapping it opens the
developer's [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/michelemi) page in your web
browser. The game itself sends nothing to Ko-fi and receives nothing
back. Anything you do on that page, such as making a donation, is
handled by Ko-fi under its own
[privacy policy](https://more.ko-fi.com/privacy). A donation is entirely
voluntary and does not unlock anything in the game.

## Children

Nice Dice is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from
anyone, it collects no data from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version of the
game adds a feature that handles data, the updated policy will be posted
on this page with a new effective date.
